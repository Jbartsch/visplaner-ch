# Product wedge (Jonas / EddA) — 2026-10-05

NOT a Dutch-style "where am I legally allowed" colour clone.

## Core job
1. Check **what permit you need** for a spot
2. Show **where to buy it**

## Pain
Switzerland is cantonal-fragmented — nobody knows the rules (Patent vs Pacht vs Freiangel; different shops per canton).

## MVP flow
map → select water → show required patent / Pacht / Freiangel type → buy link (eFJ / cantonal shop / Pächter)

## Scope
- Cantons: **ZH + BE** (mock and/or real GIS)
- Strong disclaimer: informational only, **not** legal permission
- Legend may help, but primary UX is **permit-needed + purchase path**
